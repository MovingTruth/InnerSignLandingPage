Today · look inward

A reading that belongs to you and this day.

Give Inner Sign one birth date. It meets a stable personal foundation with the changing character of the day, in a short reflection you can take or leave.

Stable in youYour sun sign and its Inner Sign third, your Chinese zodiac animal, and its element.
Alive in the dayThe Universal Day Number, with its arithmetic shown, and the Moon’s current phase.
Fresh, not randomThe same birth date on the same day always returns the same reading.
Every part opens furtherTap a sign, an animal, the day number, or the Moon to open a fuller explanation. Every sign and constellation result and all eight Moon phases continue into a sourced Explore page; sourced pages for the Chinese zodiac animals and the day numbers are still being added.
✦ Two ways to read the sky

Traditional 12. Astronomical 13.

Read your sign through the familiar twelve-sign zodiac, or through the thirteen constellations the Sun actually crosses—Ophiuchus included. Switch lenses inside any reading and see what changes for your birthday. Only the sign changes; the rest of the reading holds still.

Neither lens is presented as the single proven truth. Curiosity is the feature.

My Sky · look upward

The sky above you, calculated on your device.

Choose a viewpoint—a single location snapshot, or a city picked from more than 34,000 stored offline—and Inner Sign works out what it can from there, without a network connection.

Where Antares standsThe bright red heart of Scorpius: above or below your horizon, which direction, and how high.
Finding AndromedaWhere M31, our nearest major galactic neighbour, sits in your sky, what it takes to actually see its faint haze, and a direct path to its finding chart in Explore.
The age of lightHow long the sunlight reaching you has travelled, the full journey of moonlight from Sun to Moon to you, and the roughly 2.5 million years Andromeda’s light has spent reaching you.
Every eclipse of the yearSolar and lunar, worldwide, with the ones visible from your viewpoint gathered first.
The year’s turning pointsEach primary Moon phase, the equinoxes, and the solstices for the year you choose.
◇ Explore · follow the connection

Twenty-one constellations, charted from the sources.

All thirteen constellations on the Sun’s path, and eight neighbours beyond it—Cetus, Orion, Canis Major, the two Bears, Cassiopeia, Andromeda, and Crux. Each guide is drawn from catalogued star positions inside its official boundary, shows how long each featured star’s light has been travelling, and includes a complete star list for anyone who would rather read than look. Andromeda’s chart also marks the Andromeda Galaxy, M31.

  • A knowledge map that shows how constellations, events, people, and traditions connect—also available as a plain list.
  • An interactive Moon lesson: move the Moon around the Earth and watch all eight phases form.
  • The Moon, the seasons, solstices, and equinoxes, each explained and linked to its sources.
  • Astronomy, history, cultural tradition, astrology, and Inner Sign’s own interpretation, always labelled as which is which.
Your Calendar

The sky’s dates, only if you ask.

Set a reminder for a single event, turn on alerts for the year’s Moon phases, seasons, and eclipses, or add the whole year to a separate Inner Sign calendar in Apple Calendar in one step. Adding it twice won’t create duplicates, and Inner Sign never reads your other events.
